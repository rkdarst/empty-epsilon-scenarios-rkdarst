## Rules for whoever turns this into an outline

1. Every place, ship and organisation you use must appear in §3 or be invented
   and added to a "new terms" list at the top of the outline. Never silently
   invent a name.
2. Do not present a guess as a fact. Where §1 is silent, mark the assumption as
   an **open question** in the outline, and prefer making it a tuning knob.
3. §2 overrides your taste. If the brief's feel conflicts with what you think is
   fun, the brief wins and you flag it.
4. §4 must be answered in the outline, item by item, with either a decision or a
   question back. An unanswered "Ask" blocks the code stage.
5. State count and distance in the units the engine has: sectors (20u each) and
   displayed units. A mission "with a lot of travelling" must say how many
   sectors, or it is not a decision.
6. Every objective must have an entry condition, an exit condition and a
   never-stuck argument. If you cannot write the never-stuck argument, the
   objective is not designed yet.
