================================================================
 scenario_42_feverrun - "Fever Run" - BRIEF
================================================================

Warning: fully AI generated scenario, use with caution.

TL;DR
-----
A one-hour story mission for a NEW crew. Sedgeon Colony has a fever
outbreak and its vaccine is overdue. The crew must reach a relay
station, work out which of two broadcasting ships is the real
(and missing) medical courier - the other one is a disguised pirate
decoy - collect all three vaccine cases, and deliver them to the
colony. Two short, easy fights happen on the way. Nothing here is
tuned to kill a careful crew; the fights are side events to the
story, not the story.

The story, in order (7 beats)
-----------------------------
1.  BRIEFING at Meridian Starbase (start): ship log + hail explain
    the errand. Objective: fly to Coldfield Relay.
2.  ARRIVAL at Coldfield Relay (within 6u): the relay reveals the
    problem - courier POLARIS went missing with vaccine case C near
    sector E8. Two ships are broadcasting there. One lies.
3.  TRIAGE (the puzzle): science scans settle it.
      - The ship answering to "POLARIS" scans as the real, crippled
        medical courier: hail her, verify credentials, receive the
        MEDICAL ACCESS CODES. Her case (CASE C) floats adrift next
        to her - fly over it to collect (a physical pickup).
      - The ship answering to "COLDFIELD-2" claims to be a relay
        tug. Scanning it reveals an Exuari hull - bait. Approaching
        it within 2.5u springs an ambush: 2 weak Exuari raiders
        attack. (Trap first, scan later = the fight happens; it is
        survivable and optional in every other way.)
4.  LOADING: return to Coldfield Relay. The medical locker opens
    with the codes; come alongside (within 1.4u) and the crane
    loads CASES A and B.
5.  FINAL LEG: warp to Sedgeon Colony across a nebula dust lane.
6.  AMBUSH AT THE COLONY (side event): within 10u of the colony,
    2 Exuari raiders spawn and attack THE COLONY, not the crew.
    The crew defends their delivery: kill them before they crack
    the station. They are weak; the colony tanks for a while.
7.  DELIVERY: come alongside Sedgeon (within 1.4u), hail the med
    unit, hand over three cases. Victory. Epilogue in the ship log.

What each station does
----------------------
- HELM:      routes, warp hops, the fly-over pickup of case C,
             holding station alongside two stations, the nebula
             crossing, not ramming the rock cluster off the lane.
- WEAPONS:   two-to-three short fights vs 2x "Adder MK4" raiders
             each (trap ambush, colony wave). Nothing bigger.
- SCIENCE:   the triage puzzle - two ships to scan, scan results
             flip their descriptions and unlock/avoid plot; link a
             probe or two if the crew wants the raw plot.
- RELAY:     all station hails (Meridian, Coldfield, Sedgeon),
             the courier hail, waypoints for the route, and free
             missile/probe restocks at Meridian and Coldfield.
- ENGINEERING: power for the warp hops, shield/repair work during
             the two fights, normal damage control.

Balance & settings
------------------
- Difficulty setting "Normal" (default) = as described.
- "Story": the crew's beams one-shot anything they hit (buff
  applied per hull in update(), so late joiners are covered).
- Crew hull: starts in an "Atlantis"; any stock hull works (the
  scenario installs warp on hulls that lack it).
- Rep is not used; restocks are free. Time pressure is fiction,
  not a timer.

Anti-softlock (the mission can always finish)
---------------------------------------------
- Codes lost (Polaris destroyed, or triage never done): 300 s after
  arrival the relay itself produces an override key (notify + unlock).
- Out-of-order play is safe: a crew that triages the beacons BEFORE
  ever visiting the relay (codes taken at phase 1) still finishes -
  the phase machine is order-proof, and
  tests/scenario_42_feverrun.early-codes.autotest.lua guards it:
    AUTOTEST=tests/scenario_42_feverrun.early-codes.autotest.lua \
      ./tools/func_test.sh scenarios/scenario_42_feverrun.lua
- Case C container destroyed: a drop-watch respawns it 15 s later.
- Colony destroyed before delivery (wave, friendly fire, GM removal - any
  cause, any phase): defeat is declared; no hang.
  tests/scenario_42_feverrun.defeat.autotest.lua guards it:
    AUTOTEST=tests/scenario_42_feverrun.defeat.autotest.lua \
      ./tools/func_test.sh scenarios/scenario_42_feverrun.lua
- Coldfield Relay destroyed before cases A+B are loaded: defeat is declared
  (the medical locker and crane are gone with it). After loading, its loss is
  survivable - the delivery happens at Sedgeon.
- Mid-phase failures (anti-softlock test (e)): Polaris destroyed before the
  codes exist -> the relay override re-acquires them; case C shot to pieces ->
  the drop-watch respawns it.
  tests/scenario_42_feverrun.lost-case.autotest.lua guards both:
    AUTOTEST=tests/scenario_42_feverrun.lost-case.autotest.lua \
      ./tools/func_test.sh scenarios/scenario_42_feverrun.lua
- All crew ships destroyed: defeat; no hang.

Player communication (authoring/player-communication.md audit)
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Push: every transition, scan, trap and defeat goes through the notify queue
  (retry while a player is in a dialog).
- Pull: Meridian answers "What are my current orders?" with a per-phase line
  (fr_orders); Coldfield's page restates its own state and can "Mark the next
  objective on our nav" as a waypoint (aimed between the two broadcasters
  during triage, so it never spoils the puzzle).
- Persist: every beat is mirrored into the ship log (yellow/red/green tags) -
  all writes flow through the fr_log(msg, color) choke point (never a raw
  addToShipLog), which is what makes the channel spy-able in a driver
  (ee-skills/testing/communication-testing.md).
- Recovery: 240 s with no progress -> a station re-sends a hint naming the
  next step (FR.idle_s knob; never talks over an active beat).
